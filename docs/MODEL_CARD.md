# RuseTect model card

Generated by `research/build_report.py` from `research/out/*.json`; do not edit by hand.

## What it is

A per-payment money-laundering and mule risk score for bank transfers, served by one Rust binary (`rusetect-server`).
Risk = max(isotonic-mapped cascade risk, isotonic-mapped format-free XGBoost risk). The cascade is an XGBoost gate (L1) and the mean of
XGBoost and LightGBM (L2) on 31 causal account-history features. A 24 hour payer-to-payee aggregation raises the risk of split payments.
The format-free model is trained on the training parts of every payment dataset (bank AML, card, wallet, and IEEE-CIS real card fraud).
Bands: verify, hold, block (thresholds in `service/models/hardening.json`, set to the cascade's validation alert rates; the maps are
step functions, so the served bands run slightly above that budget, see the table).

## Intended use

- Decision support for analysts: rank payments for review, hold or step-up verification. Not an automatic blocking system.
- Shadow-mode pilots on a bank's own labelled data before any decision is taken on its output.

## Out of scope

- Automatic account closure, credit or customer-facing decisions.
- Any population it has not been measured on: performance on another bank's data is unknown until measured there.

## Training data

Cascade: IBM AMLworld HI-Small and LI-Small training weeks (synthetic, CDLA-Sharing-1.0; `cascade_config.json` `trained_on`).
Format-free second opinion: the training parts of 13 payment datasets (bank AML, card, wallet; IEEE-CIS has real card fraud labels).
Every split is chronological: train, validation (calibration and thresholds only), test. No real customer data.
The datasets and their audit: `docs/DATASET_AUDIT.md` and `docs/research/research_review.html`.

## Known limitations

- All payment training data is synthetic. Scores on real bank data are unmeasured.
- The HI-Small test part has been read by many studies, some designed after earlier test results; the served model needs one
  confirmation on data no study has read (a new generator run or a bank's shadow pilot). The pre-registered fresh holdout on a new generator (IBM AMLSim) gave: does not transfer (Fresh holdout above); a new run of the training generator and a bank's shadow pilot remain open.
- Generalisation across generators is weak. The served cascade zero-shot ROC-AUC on SAML-D 0.565; a research model (29 features plus 13 indicators) trained on all other datasets, leave-one-out ROC-AUC (same-generator siblings stay in training, which flatters the IBM sets): hismall 0.991, himed 0.990, hi_large 0.882, li_small 0.976, li_medium 0.976, li_large 0.863, samld 0.803, amlgentex 0.497, banksim 0.940, sparkov 0.751, paysim 0.349, tabformer 0.458, ieee 0.435.
- Spread-out structuring still evades most per-payment and pair checks; account-level alerts are the next step. The structuring
  numbers come from 97 laundering payments in one window, so a few payments move them by points.
- The format attack flips the payment-format field of each laundering payment; the format-free model cannot read that field, so it
  is robust to it by construction. Attacks aimed at the format-free model itself are covered only by the evasion suite (amounts,
  delays, splits, new mule accounts), where block recall stays low.
- The multi-domain second opinion is weaker than the HI-Small-only one on the two largest bank-AML sets (test ROC-AUC HI-Large 0.980 to 0.947, LI-Large 0.953 to 0.866); its adoption rule had no per-dataset guard.
- Reasons are TreeSHAP contributions of the model that set the risk (the L2 XGBoost half, or the format-free model); the LightGBM half is not explained.
- A flagged payment can also carry a "Temporal motif" reason (fan-in, fan-out, cycle back to the payer, scatter-gather, pass-through chain) read from the 24 h account windows; the idea is TempME's (Chen and Ying, NeurIPS 2023). It is not a model output, has impact 0 and never changes the score; in-memory mode only (`docs/decision.md` 8z).
- Serverless deployments (Vercel) keep state per instance; use the Redis deployment (`deploy/docker-compose.yml`) for shared state.

## Governance

- Every model change is adopted only under a rule written in its script and decided on validation before test is read (`research/*.py`);
  `docs/decision.md` lists which scripts were committed before their results and the one study that decided on test.
- Thresholds and calibration are fitted on validation only; evaluations stay chronological.
- The Rust engine matches the Python reference: the cascade to within 3e-8 (tests in `crates/rusetect-server/tests`); the hardened risk to rounding and the attack-stream block decisions exactly.
- Fail-closed configuration: the server refuses to start without analyst keys and production secrets, or an explicit demo flag.
